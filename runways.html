Ranking runways within airports

Which runway is longest at a facility, and how can we keep the other runways in view?

At FAA site 00108., runway 01/19 measures 2,002 ft. Runway 07/25 measures 1,104 ft. Ranking within each site puts those two records first and second at that facility. A ranking over the entire national file would answer a different question.

Method and limits

I partitioned the data by facility and ordered runways by length within each partition. The result retained all 23,174 input rows. I cross-checked the calculated ranks against an independent pandas calculation. The FAA snapshot describes physical infrastructure. Runway length alone cannot establish a cause of flight cancellations.
