The shape of London bike demand

Does the holiday difference look the same at breakfast and at lunch?

Across the sample, non-holidays averaged 1,152 rentals per recorded hour and holidays averaged 770. The morning gap was especially wide. At 08:00 the means were 2,941 and 301 respectively. At noon the direction reversed.

Method and limits

I grouped the hourly counts by holiday flag and clock hour and calculated a mean per recorded hour. The sample spans January 2015 through January 2017, with 384 holiday hours and 17,030 other hours. Weather, season and weekdays can also affect demand. The hourly comparison describes the pattern; it does not isolate a holiday effect.
