Which city looks most expensive, and what changes when you look at the typical sale?

Davis had the highest unfiltered median sale price, $889,000. In Athens, the mean price was $491,377. The median was $370,000. A buyer looking at the mean alone would see a price $121,377 above the middle transaction.
